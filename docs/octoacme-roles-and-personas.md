# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads oversee quality assurance activities, test planning, and acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop comprehensive test plans aligned with feature scope
- Coordinate manual QA testing and quality assessments
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria
- Identify, triage, and track defects
- Coordinate with developers on quality issues and test coverage improvements
- Review and approve test automation strategies
- Conduct regression testing and quality sign-offs before releases

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Reduce production defect rates and improve customer satisfaction
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage and quality metrics
- Enable fast feedback loops and rapid iteration

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement (quality inputs)
- PR reviews and testing updates
- Daily standups with quality status
- QA reports and defect metrics
- Release readiness assessments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** on test coverage, defect resolution, and quality standards
- Coordinates with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria clarity and quality expectations
- Partners with **Project Managers** on timeline impacts related to quality issues
- Supports **Technical Architects** on test design for complex systems

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors are executives or business leaders who provide business context, approve project scope, and make go/no-go decisions on projects. They represent organizational interests and ensure projects align with business strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and desired outcomes for projects
- Approve project charters and scope statements
- Provide resource commitments (budget, team allocation)
- Make escalation decisions on conflicts and trade-offs
- Approve scope changes and project baseline updates
- Monitor project health and business impact
- Remove organizational blockers and barriers

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Manage organizational risk and resource allocation
- Maximize return on investment (ROI)
- Maintain alignment with business strategy and priorities

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and gate reviews
- Monthly or quarterly status updates
- Executive escalation meetings
- Decision approvals on scope and resource changes
- Business impact assessments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Provides strategic direction to **Project Managers** and **Product Managers**
- Approves resource requests from the delivery team
- Receives escalations from **Project Managers** on critical issues
- Reviews business metrics and outcomes from **Product Managers**

---

## Technical Architect/Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical direction, lead system design decisions, and ensure architectural alignment across components. They guide technology choices and help the team navigate complex technical challenges.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and architecture discussions
- Review and approve architecture proposals from development teams
- Identify technical risks, bottlenecks, and design trade-offs
- Guide technology selection and engineering standards
- Coordinate cross-component integration and dependencies
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Document architecture decisions and technical guidelines

### Goals
- Maintain high system quality and architectural coherence
- Reduce technical debt and improve system maintainability
- Enable scalability, performance, and reliability
- Accelerate development by providing clear technical direction

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Design documentation and decision logs
- Dependency coordination across teams
- Code review guidance on architectural aspects
- Technical spike planning and investigation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates closely with **Developers** on design and implementation decisions
- Works with **Project Managers** on timeline implications of architectural changes
- Partners with **QA/Testing Leads** on test architecture for complex systems
- Advises **Product Managers** on technical feasibility of features

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies and processes, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile best practices. They optimize team efficiency and foster continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Track velocity and predict delivery capability
- Identify and remove process blockers and team impediments
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Monitor team health and engagement
- Manage sprint retrospectives and action item tracking
- Ensure Definition of Done is understood and followed

### Goals
- Optimize team efficiency and delivery velocity
- Improve delivery predictability and reliability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Reduce cycle time and accelerate time-to-market

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching and mentoring
- Retrospective facilitation and action item follow-up
- Team health assessments and feedback
- Velocity and burndown reports

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports all roles by removing process impediments
- Coaches **Developers** on sprint practices and sustainable pace
- Works with **Project Managers** on schedule and resource management
- Facilitates collaboration between **Product Managers** and the delivery team

---

## Product Owner

### Role Summary
Product Owners own the product backlog and work closely with Product Managers to translate business requirements into actionable, prioritized work items. They serve as the primary interface between business/customer needs and the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and continuously refine the prioritized product backlog
- Write user stories with clear acceptance criteria
- Clarify requirements and answer questions from the development team
- Accept completed work and validate it meets acceptance criteria
- Manage product backlog dependencies and sequencing
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and validate requirements
- Communicate product priorities and vision to the team

### Goals
- Ensure backlog reflects current customer needs and business priorities
- Maintain clear, actionable priorities to enable rapid development cycles
- Reduce rework and scope ambiguity
- Maximize the value delivered per sprint

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Daily standups (brief updates on priorities)
- Backlog grooming and story estimation
- Acceptance discussions and sign-offs
- Stakeholder and customer feedback sharing

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Product Managers** on roadmap and prioritization
- Coordinates with **Developers** on requirement clarity and acceptance criteria
- Aligns with **QA/Testing Leads** on test scenarios and acceptance validation
- Supports **Scrum Masters** in backlog management

---

## Operations/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage deployment infrastructure, release processes, and production monitoring. They ensure reliable, scalable, and secure deployment pipelines that enable frequent, safe releases.

### Responsibilities
- Configure and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage deployment environments (staging, production, etc.)
- Monitor production system health, performance, and availability
- Coordinate and execute deployments and rollbacks
- Manage infrastructure scaling and capacity planning
- Automate deployment and operational tasks
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and safe deployments
- Maintain high system uptime and availability
- Reduce deployment risk and mean time to recovery (MTTR)
- Optimize infrastructure costs and performance

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination and deployment planning
- Deployment windows and go/no-go decisions
- Incident response and post-incident reviews
- Infrastructure and capacity planning discussions
- Deployment status and production metrics

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinates with **Developers** on deployment requirements and CI/CD integration
- Works with **Project Managers** on release timing and deployment schedules
- Communicates production status to **QA/Testing Leads** and stakeholders
- Supports **Technical Architects** on infrastructure design decisions

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security and compliance requirements. They identify and mitigate security risks throughout the project lifecycle and maintain organizational regulatory compliance.

### Responsibilities
- Review and define security requirements for projects
- Conduct threat analysis and security risk assessments
- Approve security design and architecture decisions
- Coordinate security testing and vulnerability assessments
- Track compliance obligations and regulatory requirements
- Review and approve security-related code changes
- Maintain security policies and incident response procedures

### Goals
- Protect organizational and customer data from security threats
- Reduce security risk and compliance violations
- Ensure regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, etc.)
- Enable secure-by-default development practices

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments and threat analysis
- Security design reviews and architecture approvals
- Compliance audits and regulatory assessments
- Incident response and post-incident reviews
- Security training and awareness updates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works across all roles to integrate security into delivery
- Partners with **Developers** on secure coding practices and vulnerability remediation
- Collaborates with **Technical Architects** on security design and infrastructure hardening
- Advises **Project Managers** on security-related timeline impacts
- Supports **QA/Testing Leads** on security testing strategies

---

## Cross-Role Dependencies & Collaboration Model

The following diagram shows how these personas interact in a typical project delivery:

- **Product Manager** & **Product Owner** define what to build
- **Project Manager** coordinates the delivery timeline and resources
- **Stakeholder/Sponsor** provides business direction and removes blockers
- **Technical Architect** guides technical decisions and design
- **Developers** implement features with **QA/Testing Lead** validating quality
- **Operations/DevOps Engineer** ensures safe, reliable deployments
- **Security/Compliance Officer** validates security and regulatory requirements
- **Scrum Master** optimizes team processes and removes impediments

Success requires clear communication, shared ownership, and regular synchronization across these roles.
